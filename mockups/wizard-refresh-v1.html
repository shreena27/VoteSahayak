What changed

  • 1Check glyph on picked chips (Q1 only). As shipped, a picked chip differs from an unpicked one by fill color and a weight bump alone. A 12px check inside the chip gives a second, non-color cue for "picked", which matters on a multi-select where several chips can be on at once. It uses the chip's own --on-accent; no new token. Q2 is single-select and auto-advances on tap, so its chips never sit in a picked state and get no glyph. Both columns share one state: tap chips, Continue and Back, or use the Q1 / Q2 control.

Considered and left alone

  • ·No step indicator. Wizard.css records the decision ("no progress bar, implies bureaucratic length") and the content confirms it: four of five tasks are one question, this one is two. "Step 1 of 2" would be clutter, not signal. Confirmed with the user.
  • ·Preflight box unchanged. The ActionCard's "bolder figures" move needs a real number or key:value to bold; the preflight is one prose sentence with no figure in it, and its document names are already the whole sentence. Bolding fragments of it would also mean markup inside a content string in tasks.json, which is a content change, not a visual one.
  • ·Grouping unchanged. The screen already has exactly one grouped block (preflight) and one question; there is nothing scattered to gather.
  • ·Chip size, hint copy, Continue, slide-in, stacked Q2 layout: unchanged. Each already does one job in one place.
