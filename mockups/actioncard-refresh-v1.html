Form Guide · Form 8

Update your address — Form 8

You moved, so your voter registration needs to move with you. This is normal and common — Form 8 covers exactly this, no other form needed.
Timeline Usually a few weeks. In one documented case (a former diplomat's public account) it took up to 6 months — treat that as a possibility, not a guarantee, and apply as early as you can.
Why applications like this get rejected
Not "ordinarily resident" — about 12–15% of cases (research estimate, not an official figure)
Documents needed
  • Proof of your new address (an updated Aadhaar, a recent utility bill, or a rent agreement)Any one of these
  • Your existing Voter ID (EPIC) card, if you have one

Two screens

  1. 5Split after "Documents needed". Screen 1 (Understand & prepare): tag, headline, Listen, meaning, "Show details" (meta block + rejection reasons, closed by default), the document checklist open with its ring, and the app's real .btn-primary Continue. Screen 2 (Steps): the app's real .app-header .back with a "Steps" title, all five steps fully shown, the seal, the footer (source, verified date, helpline), and Share on WhatsApp. Continue and Back are wired; the Screen 1 / Screen 2 control above does the same.
  2. 4"Show details" stays on screen 1 for the secondary information only. The checklist itself is no longer behind a toggle because it is the point of that screen.

The three approved moves

  1. 1Checklist progress. A 20px ring plus "1 of 2 ready" on the "Documents needed" heading, driven by the real checked count (tap a box to try it). A ring rather than a bar: it echoes the round seal and the step numerals, and a full-width bar would read as an app progress bar cutting across the receipt.
  2. 2One meta block. Timeline, Verified date and Source in a single dashed-border box, in the position the timeline row used to hold. The dashed line is the receipt's existing vocabulary, so it reads as part of the paper, not a card on a card.
  3. 3Bolder keys and figures. Row labels go from muted 400 to secondary 600; the figures that decide something for the reader (6 months, 12–15%, 1 Sep 2026, 1950, 1 of 2) are set at 700 with tabular figures, using the tabular-nums rule already on body.

Copy pass (less text-heavy)

  1. TTimeline split into four short sentences, same facts, same 6-month figure: "Usually a few weeks. In one documented case, a former diplomat reported it took up to 6 months. Treat this as possible, not certain. Apply as early as you can."
  2. RRejection tag keeps every word. The caveat "(research estimate, not an official figure)" drops to a second line at 10px, weight 500, muted ink, so it reads as a footnote under the 12–15% clause rather than equal-weight text.
  3. MMeaning paragraph left as-is. No trim was found that keeps both the warmth and every fact ("normal and common", "no other form needed"), so it was not forced.
  4. DDocument 2: "Your existing Voter ID (EPIC) card, if you have one" became "Your Voter ID (EPIC) card, if you have one". "Existing" repeated what "if you have one" already says. No other label, heading or step lost a word: each remaining candidate carried a number, caveat or scope qualifier ("any one of", "exactly as it appears", "official").
